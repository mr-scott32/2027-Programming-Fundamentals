# Activities

1. **Explain** why the **Agile** model would be the most appropriate approach for developing a health and fitness application for mobile phones.

{% hint style="warning" %}
## **Explain:**

* Relate cause and effect.
* Make the relationships between things evident.
* Provide why and/or how.
* In this case, you must not only define the agile model, but also explain **why** it is the appropriate approach.
{% endhint %}

2. **Discuss** the **advantages** and **disadvantages** of using the Waterfall and Agile methodologies in software development. In your response, consider factors such as project requirements, flexibility, risk management, and stakeholder involvement.

{% hint style="warning" %}
## **Discuss**

Identify issues and provide points for and/or against.

In this case, you must consider **both advantages AND disadvantages.**

Use the examples above given to you.
{% endhint %}

3.  A software development company has been hired to create a learning management system (LMS) for a high school. The client (the school principal) provided a list of requirements. The team then began working on the project in isolation for three months and is now ready for demonstration. However, when the principal sees the system, there is a major problem. She is unhappy because the software does not match her expectations, and key features are missing.

    Identify the software development approach used.&#x20;

Propose and **justify** an alternative software development approach that would have avoided the problem.

{% hint style="warning" %}
## Justify

Support an argument or conclusion.

In this case, you must propose and define the appropriate development approach to avoid the issue.

You must also provide evidence **from the scenario and features of your development approach** to support your conclusion.
{% endhint %}
